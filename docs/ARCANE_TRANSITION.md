# First Supernatural Transition — Arcano

Status: **major design draft v1**

This is one of the most important emotional turns in the game.

Until this point, the player has learned to treat Merge Discovery as a broad but mostly natural/conceptual universe builder.

Arcano should reveal:

> The universe was not limited to ordinary reality. It only looked that way because you had not yet learned how to interpret the impossible.

## Design goals

The first supernatural transition must:

- pay off earlier unexplained experiments;
- revalue old elements;
- reveal at least one previously invisible Set;
- feel earned rather than random;
- teach that progression can change what an old pair means;
- open a large new content space without invalidating previous logic.

## Core structure

The transition has three acts.

### Act 1 — Foreshadowing

During normal progression, a few intuitive but currently impossible pairs produce authored anomalies.

Preferred examples:

- Lupo + Luna → anomaly
- Uccello + Fuoco → anomaly
- Specchio + Notte → possible anomaly, if Specchio exists by Humanity

Only a small number should be active.

The player archive records them without future names.

Example:

> Lupo + Luna  
> Reazione instabile  
> La combinazione sembra completa, ma qualcosa impedisce al risultato di prendere forma.

No “licantropo” spoiler.

### Act 2 — The idea of myth becomes real

During Humanity/Cultura, the player discovers concepts that humans create around the world:

Candidate chain:

- Umano + Tempo → Storia
- Storia + Immaginazione → Leggenda
- Leggenda + Tempo → Mito

Exact recipes depend on the Humanity content map.

`Leggenda` or `Mito` becomes a bridge between ordinary reality and Arcano.

At the appropriate progression eligibility:

`Energia + Leggenda → Magia`

or a refined equivalent becomes the preferred first Arcano unlock trigger.

Why this is strong:

- Energia is ancient and familiar;
- Leggenda is a late human/cultural concept;
- the pair means “belief/story gains force”;
- Magia emerges from the relationship between the real universe and the stories built inside it.

This exact recipe is still provisional, but the structural idea is preferred.

### Act 3 — Old anomalies wake up

Immediately after Magia is discovered:

1. hidden Set **Magia** appears;
2. catalog expands;
3. old compatible elements receive `Nuove possibilità`;
4. anomaly archive changes state;
5. one old anomaly can be revisited.

Example:

`Lupo + Luna`

Previously:

> Reazione instabile

After Arcano unlock:

> Può essere stabilizzata.

Player performs it again.

Result:

> **Licantropo**  
> Nuovo Set scoperto: **Creature fantastiche**

This creates a second reveal immediately from an old memory.

The emotional payoff is:

> I tried this hours ago. The game remembered.

## Why not unlock fantasy by level alone

Avoid:

> Livello 37 raggiunto. Magia sbloccata.

This would waste the entire anomaly system.

Level should make the transition eligible, but the player should perform the meaningful discovery.

## Recommended gating

Arcano eligibility can require:

- minimum Discovery Level;
- meaningful progress in Umanità/Cultura;
- at least one myth/legend concept;
- optionally at least one archived Arcano-compatible anomaly.

However, an anomaly must not be mandatory if the player never happened to try one.

Fallback:

Once eligible, a Cultural clue can guide the player toward the Magia trigger.

## Anomaly categories

By Arcano, anomaly types should be clearer internally.

### Mythic anomaly

Ordinary element pair that can later create a fantastic creature/object.

Examples:

- Lupo + Luna
- Uccello + Fuoco

### Dimensional anomaly

Pair reserved for much later Impossibile content.

Should remain unresolved even after Magia.

### Spiritual anomaly

Pair reserved for Invisibile.

May react differently from mythic anomalies.

The UI does not need to reveal these categories early.

They are authoring metadata.

## Old-element reactivation

Arcano should deliberately add recipes involving early content.

Priority ingredients:

- Luna
- Stella
- Fuoco
- Acqua
- Vento
- Ombra
- Foresta
- Fungo
- Animali
- Libro
- Specchio
- Metallo

This is critical.

The player should not feel that Era I–III ingredients became obsolete.

## Candidate first fantastic discoveries

These are design candidates, not yet canonical.

### Licantropo

Preferred:

`Lupo + Luna → Licantropo`

Strong because the association is universally legible.

### Fenice

Preferred:

`Uccello + Fuoco → Fenice`

Strong, simple and visually rewarding.

Could be an anomaly before Arcano.

### Unicorno

Possible:

`Cavallo + Magia → Unicorno`

This is a direct post-unlock recipe rather than an anomaly.

### Sirena

Possible:

`Umano + Oceano + future modifier`

Pair-only alternative needs careful design.

Avoid arbitrary `Umano + Pesce` unless the tone embraces very literal mashups.

### Drago

Should not be one of the first trivial fantasy recipes.

Drago deserves a more satisfying chain.

Possible ingredients later:

- Serpente
- Fuoco
- Volo/Vento
- Magia

This may be a good candidate for the later three-part experiment mechanic or multi-step precursor.

## Magia Set candidate content

The first Magia Set should include concepts and objects rather than only fantastic creatures.

Candidates:

- Magia
- Incantesimo
- Maledizione
- Incanto
- Pozione
- Grimorio
- Amuleto
- Portale
- Cristallo magico
- Runa
- Bacchetta
- Elisir

Some require Humanity materials such as Libro, Metallo, Vetro or Erbe.

This is desirable because Arcano should recombine the whole game, not form an isolated fantasy island.

## Tone shift

Art and interface should signal Arcano without changing games.

Suggested changes:

- slightly richer violet/iridescent accents;
- anomaly particles become stable geometric motifs;
- catalog gains a previously unseen iconography layer;
- reveal audio introduces a new timbre.

Do not replace the entire UI skin.

The laboratory is the same place; the player's understanding of it changed.

## Secret rule reveal

After Arcano, the game can explicitly teach:

> Alcune combinazioni dipendono da ciò che hai imparato, non soltanto dagli elementi che possiedi.

This explains why a pair that previously failed or destabilized can now work.

## Avoiding frustration

Anomalies that become resolvable should be clearly marked.

The player should not be expected to remember months-old pairs.

Recommended:

> 2 anomalie possono essere riesaminate.

Inside the archive:

> Nuova possibilità

Still require the player to perform the experiment to reveal the result.

## Arcano completion is not the end

The player may reasonably believe Magia is the game's big twist.

That makes later Invisibile and Impossibile reveals more effective.

Do not advertise them all when Arcano appears.
